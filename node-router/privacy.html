Privacy Policy for Node Router

Effective Date: November 27, 2026

Kazi Aman ("we", "our", or "us") built Node Router as a free-to-play mobile puzzle game with optional ads and in-app purchases. This page explains what information is collected and how it is used.

1. Information We Collect

We do not require you to create an account and do not directly collect your name, email address, or precise location. Your game progress and settings are stored locally on your device.

2. Third-Party Services

Node Router uses third-party Google services that may collect device identifiers (such as Advertising ID), crash/performance diagnostics, and anonymized gameplay events to provide ads, purchases, and analytics:

  • Google AdMob & User Messaging Platform (UMP): Displays optional rewarded and interstitial ads and manages ad consent preferences (EEA/UK/Switzerland). Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Billing: Processes in-app purchases securely through Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment card details.
  • Firebase Analytics: Collects anonymized gameplay telemetry (such as level starts, completions, hint usage, ad views, and shop purchases) to improve puzzle balance and game stability.

3. Local Notifications

With your permission, Node Router schedules local reminders (such as daily puzzle or shrine alerts) directly on your device. No notification data is sent to external servers.

4. Children's Privacy

Node Router is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Your Choices

  • You can manage ad personalization or reset your Advertising ID in your Android device settings.
  • Where required by regional law, you can update your consent choices anytime via Settings → Ad Privacy Settings inside the game.
  • You can delete all local game data by clearing app storage or uninstalling the app.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at: kazi.aman@proton.me
